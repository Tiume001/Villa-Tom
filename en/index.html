The Experience

An oasis of peace between Marsala's sea and the Egadi Islands

Nestled in the quiet Marsala countryside, Villa Timea is a unique estate with 5 bedrooms (including one in a private dependance) and 4 bathrooms, where generous spaces, scenic beauty, and open-air living blend into an unforgettable vacation experience.

Its greatest highlight is undoubtedly the panoramic vantage point: from the terraces, your gaze sweeps over the sparkling sea and the Egadi Islands (Favignana, Levanzo, and Marettimo), treating you to glorious sunset vistas each evening.

Outside, the large private pool reserved exclusively for guests is the vibrant heart of the estate. Framed by palm trees and sun terraces, it is the ideal place to soak up the Sicilian sun, dive into crystal clear water, and unwind in complete serenity and absolute privacy. The diving board adds fun for friends and family alike.

For sports lovers, the private regulation tennis court reserved exclusively for guests allows you to alternate deep relaxation with spirited matches without ever having to share spaces, surrounded by a peaceful pine forest with illuminated evening paths.

Explore Marsala & Trapani

Treasures of Western Sicily

Villa Timea is the perfect launching pad to discover ancient history, nature reserves, and pristine island beaches.

🏝️

Egadi Islands (Favignana, Levanzo & Marettimo)

Visible right from the villa's terraces! The Egadi archipelago — Favignana with its turquoise coves (Cala Rossa, Cala Azzurra), picturesque Levanzo, and unspoiled Marettimo with its sea caves — is easily reached: hydrofoils depart from Marsala port just 10 minutes away by car.

⏱️ 10 min to Marsala port
🦩

Stagnone Nature Reserve & Salt Pans

Historic windmills, white salt mounds, and pink flamingos. World-famous for kitesurfing and one of Europe's most photographed sunsets, alongside the ancient Phoenician island of Mozia.

⏱️ 12 min by car
🍷

Historic Marsala Wineries

Home of the world-renowned DOC fortified wine. Tour the monumental oak barrels of Cantine Florio, Donnafugata, and Pellegrino with guided tastings and Sicilian cuisine.

⏱️ 8-12 min to top wineries
🏖️

Golden Beaches of the South Coast

Miles of equipped beach clubs and free golden sand beaches with shallow, crystal-clear water (Lido Signorino, Torrazza, Tiburon Beach).

⏱️ 15 min to the coast
🏛️

Marsala Historic Center

Stroll through the elegant pedestrian old town, discover Porta Garibaldi, the Baroque Cathedral, the Punic Ship Archaeological Museum, and lively evening wine bars.

⏱️ 8 min to town center
✈️

Nearby Airports

Quick and easy to reach: Trapani-Birgi Airport (Vincenzo Florio) with domestic and Ryanair flights is just 10 minutes away; Palermo International Airport (Falcone-Borsellino) is about 1 hour away.

⏱️ 10 min (Trapani-Birgi) • 60 min (Palermo)

Frequently Asked Questions

Useful Information about Villa Timea

Everything you need to know before booking your stay in Marsala.

Are the swimming pool and tennis court truly for exclusive use?
Yes, 100%. Both the large panoramic swimming pool with diving board and the regulation tennis court are reserved exclusively for guests staying at Villa Timea, with no sharing with anyone else.
How many guests can Villa Timea accommodate and how are the rooms arranged?
The villa comfortably sleeps up to 12 guests across 5 bedrooms (including one in a private dependance) and 4 full bathrooms, plus a spacious living room with fireplace, dining hall seating 10+, veranda, and outdoor wood-fired pizza oven.
How far is Villa Timea from the Egadi Islands ferry port, beaches, and airports?
The villa is located at Contrada Fontanelle 231 in Marsala: the historic center is around 8 minutes by car, hydrofoils to Favignana, Levanzo, and Marettimo are 10 minutes away, Trapani-Birgi Airport is 10 minutes away, the Stagnone Lagoon is 12 minutes away, and sandy southern beaches are about 15 minutes away.
What is the advantage of booking directly here instead of Airbnb or Booking.com?
By booking directly via WhatsApp, phone, or email on our official website, you avoid portal service fees, get the guaranteed best rate, and coordinate check-in, check-out, and special requests directly with the owners.
Does the villa have air conditioning, private parking, and outdoor dining areas?
Yes, bedrooms are air-conditioned and the estate offers private parking, an evening-lit Mediterranean pine grove, panoramic Egadi-view terraces, sun loungers, and an authentic stone wood-fired oven.
